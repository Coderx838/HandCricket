How to play

hand cricket, explained like a friend would

the whole game in one breath

Every ball, both sides secretly hold up 1 to 6 fingers. Then you show them at the same time.

  • Different numbers → the batter scores their own number in runs. Held up 5? Nice, 5 runs.
  • Same number → the batter is OUT. Classic hand cricket. No mercy.

that's it. that's the core. everything else is just bookkeeping.

what a match looks like

  • each team bats once: 2 overs (that's 12 balls) and 3 wickets, whichever ends first.
  • wickets left after 12 balls? use them wisely — each OUT costs one, and the next batter in your lineup walks in.
  • team 2 chases the score. target = team 1's runs + 1. Reach it and you win. Run out of balls or wickets first and you don't.
  • same score at the end? it's a tie. everybody shrugs, nobody loses.

the batting & bowling bit

  • your batter stays in until they're OUT. no running between wickets, no strike rotation — this is finger cricket, not Lord's.
  • a bowler bowls a whole over (6 balls). only between overs can the captain swap in a different bowler.
  • odd runs don't switch the strike. ever. your number, your runs, your problem.

teams, captains & the toss

  • in a room, everyone picks team A or B. the first person on a team is the captain (you can pass the crown by clicking "make captain").
  • both captains secretly call heads or tails. neither can peek — calls lock until both are in.
  • toss winner chooses to bat or bowl first.
  • then each captain sets the lineup: batting order (who's next when someone's out) and bowling order (who starts each over).
  • at the innings break, the chasing captain gets one more shot: they can re-order the batters before the chase starts.

1-player teams skip the lineup step automatically — there's nobody else to order.

no cheaters, we checked

your pick is locked in (hashed, whatever that means) before the other side's pick is revealed. so nobody can change their fingers at the last second. we're not animals.

tiny tips

  • people almost never throw the same number twice in a row. exploit that.
  • when chasing, the captain can stack the order so their best guesser bats first.
  • bowling captain: match your bowler against a batter they'll fluster. it's just rock-paper-scissors with fingers.
